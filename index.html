Privacy Policy

PartyMingle: 3 Party Games

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Your privacy matters.

PartyMingle does not require account registration. Voice recordings are processed locally on your device. The app uses Google AdMob for advertising and Apple's StoreKit for purchases. Advertising partners may process certain device and usage information as described below.

1. Overview

PartyMingle: 3 Party Games ("PartyMingle", "we", "our", or "the App") is a collection of social party games, including Alias, Imposter, and Reverse Audio.

We respect your privacy and are committed to providing transparent information about how data is handled when you use our App.

This Privacy Policy explains the App's data practices, device permissions, advertising services, purchases, and privacy controls.

2. Data Collection and Processing

PartyMingle does not require users to create an account or register to play.

We do not operate a separate server for collecting player profiles, voice recordings, or gameplay histories.

However, third-party services integrated into the App, particularly Google AdMob, may automatically collect and process certain information to provide advertising and related services.

Depending on device settings, applicable law, and the services used, this information may include:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Device and app-related identifiers.
  • Information about advertising interactions and ad performance.
  • App interactions relevant to advertising services.
  • Device information and technical characteristics.
  • Crash logs, diagnostic information, and performance data.

The exact information processed may depend on the advertising configuration, user consent choices, and applicable privacy requirements.

3. Microphone and Audio Recordings

PartyMingle includes a Reverse Audio game that uses the device's microphone.

Microphone permission is requested when necessary to use audio recording features.

Audio recordings are processed and played back locally on the device.

Voice recordings are not uploaded to developer-operated servers or sent to Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

You can control microphone permission through your device's iOS privacy settings.

If you choose to share an audio recording using a device sharing feature, the information may be handled by the app or service you select.

4. Local Data Storage

PartyMingle may store information locally on your device to support gameplay and application functionality.

This may include:

  • Game settings and preferences.
  • Selected categories and difficulty.
  • Local gameplay progress and results.
  • Advertising timing information.
  • Application configuration settings.

This gameplay information is not transmitted to developer-operated servers.

Apple StoreKit and Google advertising services may separately manage their own information as described in this Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising - Google AdMob

PartyMingle uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements in the free version of the App.

Advertisements may appear between completed games or rounds. Advertisements are not intended to interrupt active gameplay or audio recording.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process device information, approximate location, identifiers, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information.

Such data may be used for purposes including:

  • Delivering advertisements.
  • Measuring advertising performance.
  • Preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Maintaining security.
  • Improving advertising services.

The current advertising configuration requests non-personalized advertisements. Nevertheless, non-personalized advertising may still involve processing technical, device, and usage-related information.

We do not use advertising identifiers for app tracking without implementing applicable permission and disclosure requirements.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

You can also learn more about how Google uses information:

Google Business Data Privacy

6. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

PartyMingle uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices where required.

Depending on your region and applicable legal requirements, you may be presented with options to:

  • Consent to eligible advertising data processing.
  • Decline consent where applicable.
  • Review and manage available privacy choices.

Where required, you can revisit your advertising privacy choices through the privacy settings available in PartyMingle.

Declining optional advertising consent does not prevent you from playing the App's available games.

Certain advertising-related processing may still occur where legally permitted or necessary to provide the service.

Users can also manage applicable advertising and privacy permissions through their device settings.

7. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

PartyMingle offers optional in-app purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions.

These may include:

  • Remove Ads Forever - a one-time purchase that permanently removes advertisements while the purchase entitlement remains valid.
  • Premium Weekly - a weekly subscription providing Premium features and an ad-free experience.
  • Premium Yearly - a yearly subscription providing the same Premium benefits.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

We do not directly receive or store your payment card number or full payment credentials.

Apple processes purchases, manages subscription billing, and provides information required to verify purchase entitlements.

PartyMingle uses verified StoreKit purchase information to determine access to paid features.

Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through their Apple Account settings.

For more information, see: Apple Privacy Policy

8. Children's Privacy

PartyMingle is a general-audience social party game.

We do not knowingly request or collect children's names, contact information, or voice recordings through developer-operated servers.

The App may use third-party advertising services, which can process technical information as described above.

We aim to comply with applicable children's privacy requirements.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us using the email address below.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Data Processing

Such processing is governed by their applicable privacy policies, legal obligations, and relevant data protection safeguards.
