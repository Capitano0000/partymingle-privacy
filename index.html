Privacy Policy

PartyMingle: 3 Party Games

Last updated: November 21, 2026

At a glance

No account is required. Microphone recordings stay on your device. Google provides advertising and consent services; Apple handles purchases.

1. Who we are

StrayPixels Studio provides PartyMingle: 3 Party Games ("PartyMingle" or "the App"), including Alias, Find Imposter and Reverse Audio. We do not operate a server for player profiles, audio recordings or gameplay histories.

2. Information handled by the App and its providers

Game settings, player and team names, progress, results and advertising timing information are handled locally. They are not sent to developer-operated servers.

When advertising services are used, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may collect approximate location, device identifiers, product interactions, advertising data, crash data, performance data and other diagnostic data. This can include an IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad impressions or interactions. Disabling a location setting does not necessarily prevent approximate location from being inferred from an IP address.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, support advertising analytics, prevent fraud and maintain its services. Device identifiers may be associated with a user or device and used for tracking when authorized. Data handling depends on permissions, consent, region and the services involved.

3. Advertising, consent and tracking choices

Where advertising is available in the free version, ads may appear after a completed match, at a natural break between turns or teams, or after you confirm leaving an unfinished match. Ads do not interrupt active gameplay or audio recording. Active Premium Weekly or Premium Yearly subscriptions and a valid Remove Ads Forever purchase suppress all advertising. Remove Ads Forever does not unlock Premium gameplay.

Personalized advertising is permitted only when applicable App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization and regulatory permissions allow it. We require the necessary authorization before allowing tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies.

Without it, only eligible non-personalized or limited advertising may be requested, or no advertising may be available. Non-personalized advertising can still involve processing device, technical and interaction data; it does not, by itself, establish that tracking is absent.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents applicable consent messages and privacy choices. Where required, reopen Privacy Options in the App's settings to review or change those choices.

Declining optional consent does not prevent access to the available free games and does not authorize personalized advertising.

Manage or withdraw ATT permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > PartyMingle. Available controls depend on your device and account. ATT and UMP are separate permissions; granting one does not replace the other.

4. Microphone and local audio

Reverse Audio requests microphone permission for recording. Recordings are processed, reversed and played locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers or sent to Google for advertising.

Manage microphone access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple's App Store and StoreKit process purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions. The App uses verified transaction and entitlement information to provide purchased access and restore purchases.

We do not receive your payment card number or full payment credentials. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Restore Purchases is available in the App.

6. Providers, retention and your rights

Google and Apple process information under their own privacy policies and may process it outside your country. Their policies explain their retention practices, safeguards and available data controls:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Business Data Privacy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information. Use the App's privacy controls or contact us.

For information independently held by Google or Apple, use the respective provider's privacy controls and request procedures.

Local App data can be removed by deleting the App; this does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete information held separately by a provider. No electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

PartyMingle is a general-audience social game. We do not knowingly collect children's names or recordings through developer-operated servers. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us.

8. Updates and contact

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact StrayPixels Studio at straypixels.studio@gmail.com .
