Privacy Policy

PartyMingle: 3 Party Games

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

PartyMingle: 3 Party Games is a collection of social party games, including Alias, Imposter, and a reverse audio challenge.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles data and device permissions.

2. Data Collection

PartyMingle does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on developer-operated servers.

The app does not require users to create an account or register to play.

We do not collect names, email addresses, location information, or other personal identifiers through the app.

3. Microphone and Audio Recordings

PartyMingle includes a reverse audio game that uses your device's microphone.

Microphone access is requested only when necessary for audio recording features.

Audio recordings are processed and played back locally on your device.

Audio recordings are not uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

You can manage or disable microphone access at any time through your device's privacy settings.

4. Local Data Storage

PartyMingle may store game preferences, settings, and gameplay information locally on your device.

This locally stored information is used only to support game functionality and is not transmitted to the developer.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of PartyMingle does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics services that collect user data.

If advertising, analytics, or other services are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Children's Privacy

PartyMingle does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The app is designed for casual entertainment and party gaming.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's features, functionality, or privacy practices.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how PartyMingle handles your data, please contact us at:
